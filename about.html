Who we are

Focused on cleaner, better-performing solar.

OSPCS specialises in professional solar panel cleaning and maintenance for residential, commercial, industrial and solar farm clients. The team combines practical field experience with specialist cleaning equipment and safe, non-abrasive methods.

Since inception, OSPCS has cleaned more than 20,000 solar panels and built a reputation for consistent, professional service. The business also offers air-conditioner outdoor unit cleaning through its eco-Air division.
